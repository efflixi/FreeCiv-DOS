Important runtime correction:
The initial 0800/0801 physical mapping approach drew both resolutions but failed
release: CWSDPMI r7 does not implement 0801 (observed error 0x0801 and confirmed
in its supplied source). The final implementation uses managed 0501 allocation,
0508 device-page mapping, and 0502 release. It neither suppresses the error nor
leaves mappings behind until process exit.
Likewise, BIOS 4F04 restore alone left the adapter graphics configuration wrong
even when the BIOS data area reported mode 3. Final restoration explicitly sets
the original mode FIRST, restores hardware/BDA/DAC/VBE state, then legacy text
video pages. The restored-text image proves visible DOS recovery and contents.
initial-cleanup-failure.log / cleanup.png / managed-cleanup-debug.log retain
intermediate troubleshooting evidence; only final dos-vbe640/dos-vbe800 logs
and screenshots are completion evidence.
